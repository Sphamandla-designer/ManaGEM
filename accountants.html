What you can rely on

Invoices you can match without chasing the client

The job, the invoice and the payment sit on one record in ManaGEM, so what reaches Sage is what was billed.

  • Every invoice carries its Sage reference, so you can match it to the ledger.
  • Net, VAT and total travel with each invoice. Nobody re-types them.
  • Customers are tied to their Sage account codes when the client is set up.
  • Debtors are aged by customer, with the last interaction and statement date beside the balance.
  • Every list exports to CSV, Excel or PDF for your working papers.

Questions from accountants

Before you set up a client

01Does ManaGEM replace Sage?

No. ManaGEM handles enquiries, quotes, jobs, stock and invoicing, and posts invoices and products to Sage. You keep working in Sage as you do now.

02Which Sage product does it work with?

ManaGEM syncs with Sage Accounting. Ask us which Sage editions your client’s setup needs before you start.

03What does it cost my client?

ManaGEM is priced by the number of people who log in. Starter is R 1,000 a month for up to 2 users, Team is R 2,000 for up to 5, and Growing is R 3,000 for up to 10. Every plan has every module. Prices exclude VAT and are billed monthly, with no minimum term.

04My client has several companies or branches.

Talk to us. Setups with several companies or branches are planned with the team, so the structure fits how the businesses relate.

05What happens to the data if my client leaves?

It is theirs. Every list exports to CSV, Excel or PDF.
