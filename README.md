# ManaGEM website

Marketing site for ManaGEM, the job-management product built and supported by GEMIS.

## Status

`reference/ManaGEM-website-V1_1.html` is the V1 content draft: a single self-contained
page with placeholder data and inline review notes. It is the content baseline for the
new site, not the implementation. Copy, structure and positioning may still change.

## Content baseline (from V1)

Positioning: "Know which jobs make you money." Job management for contractors and
service businesses in South Africa, following each job from enquiry to invoice and
showing the real margin per job.

Page order in V1:

1. Hero with example job ticket (margin vs quote, cost breakdown, labour overrun flag)
2. How it works: Enquiry, Quote, Job, Costs, Margin
3. On site: foremen log hours and slips from a phone
4. Why it matters: three ways a full order book still loses money
5. Product tour: job margins, sales pipeline, debtors
6. Accounting: ManaGEM runs the work, Sage keeps the books
7. Support: the people who build it answer the phone
8. Pricing: by team size, every feature on every plan, field users separate
9. Customers: quote slot and proof facts
10. Free trial form and guided pilot offer
11. FAQ
12. Customer login strip and footer
