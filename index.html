Questions

Before you start

Do I have to stop using Sage?

No. ManaGEM handles customers, quotes, jobs and costs, and sends invoices and products to Sage. Your accountant keeps working in Sage as they do now. Ask us which Sage editions your setup needs.

Do my foremen need a full login?

No. Foremen and site staff can be field users. They log hours and slips from their phone and don't use up your plan's users.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan and keep everything you set up. If you don't, your account pauses and nothing is charged.

Can I bring my existing customers and products?

Yes. The AI take-on tool reads your spreadsheets and sets up customers, products and prices for you, or you can sync products from Sage. The team checks the result with you before you go live.

Can we run more than one company?

Yes. Switch between companies or branches from the same login. Each keeps its own customers, jobs, stock and books.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. Plans are monthly and you can cancel with a month's notice. Annual billing is available if you'd rather pay once a year.

Where is my data, and is it POPIA-compliant?

Your data is hosted securely, backed up daily and processed in line with POPIA. Ask us for the hosting and security summary for your records.

What happens to my data if I leave?

It's yours. Export every list to Excel or CSV before you go, and we remove it from our systems once your account closes.

Can ManaGEM be changed for how we work?

Often, yes. Because GEMIS builds ManaGEM, we can scope workflows, reports and integrations for your trade. Custom work is quoted separately from your subscription.
