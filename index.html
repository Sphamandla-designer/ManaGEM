Support and questions

Before you start

Straight answers on the trial, pricing, Sage, Xero and your data.

Still have a question? The people who build ManaGEM answer, by email in South African business hours. We reply within one working day. Email the team
01What does the free trial include?

Fourteen days with every module, and no card needed. Pick a plan when you are ready and keep everything you set up. If you do not, your account pauses and nothing is charged.

02How is ManaGEM priced?

By the number of people who log in. Starter is R 1,000 a month for up to 2 users, Team is R 2,000 for up to 5, Growing is R 3,000 for up to 10, and larger teams are quoted. Every plan has every module. Prices exclude VAT and are billed monthly.

03Do my foremen and site staff need a full login?

Every person who logs in counts as a user on your plan. Tell us who needs access and what they need to do, and we will recommend the right plan for your team.

04Do I have to stop using Sage or Xero?

No. ManaGEM handles enquiries, quotes, jobs, stock and invoicing, and posts invoices and products to Sage Accounting or Xero. Your accountant keeps working in their accounting software as they do now. Ask us how your setup connects.

05Can I bring my existing customers and products?

Yes. Send your customer list, price list and open jobs as Excel or CSV. The AI-assisted take-on maps them into ManaGEM and the team checks the result with you before you go live. Products can also be synced from Sage or Xero.

06Is there a minimum contract?

No. Plans are billed monthly with no minimum term. You can change plan when your team changes.

07Where is my data, and is it handled in line with POPIA?

Your data is hosted securely and processed in line with POPIA. Ask us for the hosting and security summary for your records.

08What happens to my data if I leave?

It is yours. Export every list to CSV, Excel or PDF before you go. Ask us how long records are kept after an account closes.

09Can we run more than one company?

Talk to us. Setups with several companies or branches are planned with the team on the larger-teams path, so the structure fits how your businesses relate.

10How do I get help once I am in?

Phone and email support from the ManaGEM team in South African business hours. You speak to the people who build the system. Email info@gemis.co.za or open a ticket.

11Can ManaGEM be changed for how we work?

Often, yes. Because GEMIS builds ManaGEM, we can scope workflows, reports and integrations for your trade. Custom work is quoted separately from your subscription.

12Can businesses outside South Africa use ManaGEM?

Not yet. ManaGEM currently supports South African companies only, with prices in rand and South African VAT.
